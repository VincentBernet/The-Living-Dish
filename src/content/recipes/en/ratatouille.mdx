## From garden to Provençal table

Ratatouille hails from southern France, where summer gardens overflow with eggplants, zucchinis, and ripe tomatoes. The name may derive from « ratatolha », an Occitan word for a stew.

Every family has its own version — some cook the vegetables separately before combining, others prefer a single pot. Either way, it's a generous dish that tastes even better the next day.
