## A bright, fragrant condiment

Red onion pickles add acidity, sweetness, and crunch to many dishes — tacos, burgers, salads, cheese boards, or charcuterie. This brine balances sugar and vinegar, while the spices (fennel, cloves, bay leaf) add aromatic depth without overpowering the onion.

The longer they rest, the more the flavors meld. Once opened, keep refrigerated and use within a week.
