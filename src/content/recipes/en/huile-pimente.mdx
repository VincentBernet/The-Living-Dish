steps:
  - Slice the garlic thinly, fry it for 4 to 5 minutes in the neutral oil until lightly colored, then set it aside.
  - Dry-toast the Sichuan pepper, then set it aside.
  - Dry-toast the peanuts and sesame seeds (skip this step if they are already toasted).
  - Toast the remaining spices (cardamom, cinnamon, black pepper, star anise and coriander seeds).
  - Roughly slice the ginger, without peeling it, and the scallions.
  - Infuse the spices in the oil over low to medium heat for 8 minutes with the ginger and scallions.
  - Meanwhile, grind the peanuts, Sichuan pepper, sesame seeds and previously fried garlic.
  - Fill the jar with the sugar, salt, ground mixture, soy sauce, black rice vinegar, gochugaru and cayenne pepper.
  - Strain the spices as you pour the hot oil into the jar.
