steps:
  - Peel the apples, quarter them, and toss with lemon juice.
  - Melt butter in an oven-safe skillet, add sugar, and caramelize over medium heat.
  - Arrange apple quarters in a rosette pattern, cook 15 minutes until tender.
  - Cover with pastry, tucking edges in, and bake at 180 °C (350 °F) for 25 minutes.
  - Rest 5 minutes, then invert onto a serving plate.

## A legend from the Sologne region

Tarte Tatin takes its name from sisters Stéphanie and Caroline Tatin, who ran the Hôtel Tatin in Lamotte-Beuvron in the 19th century. Legend has it that Stéphanie accidentally flipped an apple tart while removing it from the oven — and the result delighted her guests.

Today, this dessert ranks among the great classics of French pastry, loved for its silky caramel and tender, slow-cooked apples.
